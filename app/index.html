    Roadmap to the platinum

    Estimated time: 90–140 hours for a story-first run with the stage 7–9 sweeps — an estimate derived from the official descriptions and the cited guides, not a promise. Difficulty: moderate — no trophy in the official list demands a harder difficulty setting; the risk is the seven missable windows, not skill. This roadmap is built from the official descriptions: the chapters and missions they name come first, in order; missables are flagged at the stage where they are lost; then collectibles by their counts, activities, and cleanup to 100%.

    Missable trophies — read before you play

    Seven trophies are flagged missable. Each flag is derived from the wording of its official description — a one-time location, a choice, a fail-and-retry event — and each entry says why, when it can be missed, and the point of no return. Every flag is labelled as inference where it is one: keep a manual save before each window and a flag costs you minutes, not a playthrough.
